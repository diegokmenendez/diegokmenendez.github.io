X Energy Lab - 2in Rotating Detonation Engine

I took a 2in rotating detonation engine from an empty room to first hotfire. I owned the design, fabrication, testing, and integration of every component, from the engine itself to the facility around it. Since first hotfire, I have run the engine for various research projects.

1D Thermal Modeling

I created a simplified 1D thermal model to assess how we can expect different materials to fail. This model was then calibrated to real testing data, and it gives an insight into the chamber conditions that we would not otherwise have.
